About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple note-taking app for jotting down ideas, reminders and to-dos in seconds. It is built for students and busy people who want their notes close at hand without any clutter.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes and click the "New note" button.
  2. Type your title and the text of your note.
  3. Save the note and find it later in your notes list.

Features

  • Create and edit notes instantly
  • Search through all your notes
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers
  • Keyboard shortcuts for common actions

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
Ctrl + S Save the current note
Ctrl + F Search your notes
